Figures for the FlyRank capstone paper (Lane 3: content archetype clustering)

Data: FlyRank ML Internship dataset (warehouse build v20260703), 182,135 pages, 59 clients.
All figures are generated by the notebooks in work/notebooks/. No client names or queries appear.

fig1_split_honesty.png
  Silhouette score on train and test for a naive random split versus a split grouped by client.
  Naive test = 0.375 (55 of 59 clients appear on both sides). Grouped test = 0.335 on unseen clients.
  Source: w06_validation_audit.ipynb

fig2_where_shortfall_sits.png
  Share of pages, impressions and estimated CTR shortfall for each of the four archetypes.
  Shortfall is the sum of baseline scores. High-Reach Opportunity holds about 94% of it.
  Source: w07_action_playbook.ipynb and capstone.ipynb

fig3_k_selection.png
  Train and held-out-client silhouette for k = 3 to 9. Held-out peaks at k = 4 and k = 5 (0.336); k = 4 chosen.
  Source: w05_model.ipynb
